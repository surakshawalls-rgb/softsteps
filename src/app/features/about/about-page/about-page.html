01 OUR STORY

Soft Steps began with a family tradition.

Since 1991, our grandfather has built a carpet business rooted in skilled craftsmanship, hard work, and trust. For years, the business served customers close to home, carrying forward a tradition shaped by dedication and experience.

During the COVID-19 period, Praveen Pandey, Pradeep Vishwakarma, and Sarvesh Pandey returned home and saw an opportunity: to bring their family's craft to the world. They became the founders of Soft Steps, carrying forward their grandfather's legacy while building a brand with a global ambition.

Today, our vision is to connect the craftsmanship of Bhadohi with customers, designers, and businesses looking for carpets that combine thoughtful design, comfort, and quality.

The Making of a Carpet

What is a carpet, and how is it handcrafted?

A carpet is a woven or knotted textile made to cover and warm a floor, built from fibres such as wool, silk or cotton.

A handcrafted carpet is made by skilled artisans, not by machine. Each piece moves from design and fibre selection, to dyeing and spinning, to knotting or tufting on the loom, and finally to washing, trimming and careful inspection.

Every step is done with patience, which is why no two handmade carpets are exactly alike.
