Why contact us

Direct from our factory to your space.

Contact Soft Steps Carpets for direct-from-factory pricing and superior craftsmanship. We manage the entire manufacturing process in-house, from weaving and latexing to cutting and stitching, which removes third-party production costs. As we export directly to B2B and B2C buyers worldwide, without middlemen or intermediaries, the cost savings and strict quality control pass straight to you.

01

Choosing & Shorting Fabrics

We source premium rug fabrics directly from our local hub, working closely with our in-house manufacturing team to craft custom sizes, vibrant colors, and unique designs tailored specifically to your exact requirements.

02

In-house Designing & Drafting

Our skilled artisans conceptualize, sketch, and draft intricate carpet patterns directly within our premises, translating your creative vision into precise weaving blueprints before production even begins.

03

Guidance on the Quality Control

Every carpet receives meticulous finishing touches—including latexing, shearing, carving, and edge stitching—directly in our dedicated unit, ensuring exceptional durability, plush texture, and flawless quality

04

Final Touches & Packaging

In our dedicated finishing unit, every carpet undergoes meticulous final touches—including trimming excess pile for a smooth surface, applying durable anti-slip backing, and secure packaging—ensuring your rug arrives ready to roll out.
